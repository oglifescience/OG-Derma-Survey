QUESTION 2 (Q2) — RATING (1 to 5 Stars)
--------------------------------------------
Type: 5-star / 5-level rating

"Knowing exactly what's in your cleanser matters to me."

Rating Scale:
  ★☆☆☆☆  1 — Not at all
  ★★☆☆☆  2 — Not really
  ★★★☆☆  3 — Somewhat
  ★★★★☆  4 — Quite a lot
  ★★★★★  5 — Absolutely, it's everything

Purpose:
  Replaces Q1 (transparency driver) + Q2 (frustration with not
  understanding ingredients). Combines both into one clean signal.

--------------------------------------------
QUESTION 3 (Q3) — AGREE / DISAGREE SCALE (1 to 6)
--------------------------------------------
Type: 6-point Likert (no neutral midpoint — forces a lean)

"My skin has a specific type AND a specific concern — I want a product built for that exact combination, not a generic formula."

Scale:
  1 — Strongly Disagree  (Generic products work fine for me)
  2 — Disagree
  3 — Slightly Disagree
  4 — Slightly Agree
  5 — Agree
  6 — Strongly Agree    (I need something made for my exact combo)

Purpose:
  Captures the OG Derma hyper-specific model:
  Skin Type (oily / dry / combination) + Skin Concern (acne / pigmentation / etc.)
  = one product built for that exact pair.
  Score 4–6 → they feel "seen" by hyper-specific targeting → SKN buyer
  Score 1–3 → generalists → less aligned with the SKN concept

--------------------------------------------
QUESTION 4 (Q4) — APPEAL RATING (1 to 5)
--------------------------------------------
Type: 5-level appeal / interest scale

"How appealing is this to you: You tell us your skin type and your skin concern — we give you the product for that. No actives listed on the front. No marketing jargon. Just: this is your issue, this is your skin type, here's your solution."

Scale:
  1 — Not appealing at all     (I want full ingredient details upfront)
  2 — Slightly appealing       (some info is good, actives matter to me)
  3 — Somewhat appealing       (some info is fine, don't need everything)
  4 — Very appealing           (no jargon needed, just tell me it works)
  5 — This is exactly what I need  (skin type + concern = done, nothing more)

Purpose:
  Directly gauges interest in the SKN hyper-specific model.
  Moves beyond agreement (Q3) to actual desire/appeal.
  High scores (4–5) = strong product-market fit signal for the SKN concept.

--------------------------------------------
QUESTION 5 (Q5) — LIKELY / NOT LIKELY (1 to 5)
--------------------------------------------
Type: 5-level likelihood scale

"How likely are you to recommend this product to a friend?"

Scale:
  1 — Never
  2 — Unlikely
  3 — Maybe
  4 — Likely
  5 — Definitely

Purpose:
  Replaces Q3 (describing to a friend).
  NPS-style intent is a cleaner signal than choosing a pre-written quote.

--------------------------------------------
QUESTION 6 (Q6) — QUICK PICK (2–3 options)
--------------------------------------------
Type: Single select, short labels only

"How do you prefer to learn about your skincare?"

Options:
  📖  Read the label / details
  📱  Scan QR → watch a short video
  👩‍⚕️  Trust a derm / influencer
  ✨  I just want it to work

Purpose:
  Replaces Q4 (how do you prefer to learn the science).
  Same meaning, emoji anchors make it scannable in 2 seconds.
